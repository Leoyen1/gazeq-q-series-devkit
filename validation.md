# Engineering validation workflow

1. Record module, firmware, interface, output frame, report period, mounting, supply and host configuration.
2. Verify stationary output, timestamp continuity and packet integrity.
3. Test controlled roll, pitch and yaw motion at representative speeds.
4. Compare Q9 9-axis and 6-axis paths when magnetic heading is required.
5. Test near real motors, current paths, magnets, speakers and steel structures.
6. Repeat under representative vibration and temperature conditions.
7. Save raw logs, plots, pass/fail limits and the final selection decision.

See the [official validation methods](https://gazeq.com/imu-validation-test-methods/) and [evaluation checklist](https://gazeq.com/q9-q6-imu-engineering-evaluation-checklist/).
